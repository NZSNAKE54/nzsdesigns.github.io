Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026
HomeSwipe NZ is built with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. All your swipe decisions and notes are stored only on your device.

Who we are

HomeSwipe NZ is a mobile application developed by NZSDesigns, a sole trader based in New Zealand. The app helps New Zealand home buyers browse, shortlist, and track residential property listings.

What data we collect

HomeSwipe NZ collects no personal data from users. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact details
  • We do not collect your location or GPS data
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not use analytics or usage tracking services
  • We do not collect any financial information

Data stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to any server:

  • Your swipe decisions (liked and passed properties)
  • Personal notes you add to listings
  • Your filter preferences (region, price range, bedrooms etc.)

This data exists solely on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted.

Property listing data

HomeSwipe NZ retrieves residential property listings from the Trade Me Property API. When the app fetches listings, a standard read-only API request is made to Trade Me's servers. This request includes your selected search filters (such as region and price range) but contains no personal information about you.

Listing data is cached on our backend server for up to 5 minutes for performance purposes only and is not stored permanently. We do not modify, resell, or share any listing data obtained from Trade Me.

Trade Me's own privacy policy applies to their services and can be found at trademe.co.nz/trust-safety/privacy-policy.

Email address (optional)

If you choose to use the "Email my shortlist" feature, you will be prompted to enter an email address. This address is used solely to pre-fill your device's mail application with your shortlist. HomeSwipe NZ does not store, transmit, or have any access to your email address — the email is composed and sent entirely by your own mail application.

Third-party services

HomeSwipe NZ uses the following third-party services:

  • Trade Me Property API — to source residential property listings for New Zealand. Trade Me's privacy policy applies to their API.
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet — to display an interactive map of liked properties. No personal data is sent to OpenStreetMap. Their privacy policy can be found at osmfoundation.org.
  • Unsplash — property photos used in demo mode only. Unsplash's privacy policy applies to photo delivery.

Children's privacy

HomeSwipe NZ is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Data security

All communication between the HomeSwipe NZ app and our backend server uses HTTPS encryption. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data at risk of exposure.

Data deletion

Since all personal data (swipe decisions, notes, preferences) is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Going to your phone's Settings → Apps → HomeSwipe NZ → Clear Data
  • Uninstalling the app

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of HomeSwipe NZ after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Your rights under the Privacy Act 2020

As a New Zealand resident, you have rights under the Privacy Act 2020. Since HomeSwipe NZ does not collect personal information, there is no personal information held by us to access, correct, or delete. If you have any concerns, please contact us using the details below.
