Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026
HomeSwipe NZ is built with your privacy in mind. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or location. We use an anonymous device identifier to power app features. Notes you write are automatically scanned to remove any personal details before they are ever shared with anyone.

Who we are

HomeSwipe NZ is a mobile application developed by NZSDesigns, a sole trader based in New Zealand. The app helps New Zealand and Australian home buyers browse, shortlist, and track residential property listings. This policy applies to all users of the app.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, GPS location, or any government-issued identifiers.

Anonymous device identifier

When you first open HomeSwipe NZ, a random anonymous ID is generated for your device. This ID has no connection to your identity. It is used to save your swipe history, link you to a Partner session, and manage notification preferences. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, a new device ID is generated.

Partner sessions

The Partner feature lets two people browse listings together and see where their preferences match. A session is created on our servers and both devices are linked via a shared code. You can delete your session and all associated data at any time from within the app.

Notes and automatic PII redaction

Notes you write on listings are stored on our servers. Before any note is used for anything other than displaying it back to you, it is automatically scanned and personal information is replaced with a placeholder:

  • Names → [NAME]
  • Phone numbers → [PHONE]
  • Email addresses → [EMAIL]
  • Street addresses → [ADDRESS]

You will always see your original note within the app. The redacted version is the only version ever used outside your session.

Anonymised market research data

By using HomeSwipe NZ, you agree that your anonymised, aggregated swipe data and redacted notes may be shared with third parties (such as real estate agents and property market researchers) for market research purposes. No personal information, device identifiers, or raw notes are ever included in this data.

This data contains no device IDs, user IDs, or session IDs. It only includes listings with a minimum number of swipes so individual responses are never singled out, and only includes notes after automatic PII redaction. Recipients see totals and patterns, not individual decisions.

To opt out, email nzsdesigns.app@gmail.com.

Push notifications

If you enable new listing alerts, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token on our servers. This token is used only to deliver push notifications to your device and is never shared with third parties.

Property listing data

HomeSwipe NZ retrieves listings from the Trade Me Property API. Search requests include your selected filters but contain no personal information. Listing data is cached on our servers for up to 5 minutes only. Trade Me's privacy policy applies: trademe.co.nz.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — database provider, Asia-Pacific region. supabase.com/privacy
  • Firebase (Google) — push notification delivery only.
  • Railway — backend server hosting. No personal data stored at server layer.
  • Trade Me Property API — source of property listings.
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet — map display. No personal data sent.

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as you use the app. If you have not used the app for 12 months, your anonymous session data may be deleted.

Data deletion

You can delete your session from within the app at any time. To request full deletion of all data, email nzsdesigns.app@gmail.com and we will action it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

HomeSwipe NZ is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data security

All communication uses HTTPS encryption. Database access is controlled by row-level security policies. We do not store passwords or financial information.

Your rights

New Zealand users — under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to access and correct information we hold. Contact us with your device ID and we will assist you.

Australian users — under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles, you have similar rights of access and correction.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
